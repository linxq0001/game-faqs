# Battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes Meta Upgrades Guide

![Battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes official Steam trailer still showing fleet conflict](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2535950/257272938/d6ff79de7866ac6487268c4e4a2dad87ac35e698/movie_600x337.jpg)

Scattered Hopes is not designed around perfect first clears. Permanent upgrades are part of the intended progression, and the smartest early path is usually the one that makes future runs harder to collapse.

## Direct Answer

Prioritize permanent upgrades that improve survival and route stability before damage spikes. Fuel efficiency, Gunstar armor, and supply capacity usually give more long-term value than flashy combat boosts. Once your runs stop collapsing early, then branch into stronger damage or utility upgrades.

## 1. The Right Early Mindset

### Expect To Learn Through Losses

Early defeats are not proof that the build failed. They are information about which system broke first. Use that information to decide what your next permanent investment should fix.

### Do Not Chase Perfect Openers

If you wait for a perfect run before learning the progression system, you will improve more slowly. Stable habits plus good permanent upgrades matter more than one unusually lucky start.

## 2. Best Early Upgrade Priorities

### Survival Upgrades Come First

Fuel efficiency, extra Gunstar armor, and stronger supply reserves help almost every run regardless of fleet style. They protect the route, smooth out mistakes, and give you more room to recover from bad events.

### Combat Upgrades Come Second

Once early collapses happen less often, damage upgrades start pulling more weight. Better squadron damage or faster main-gun pressure can help shorten dangerous fights.

### Utility Upgrades Can Wait

Resource efficiency, faction smoothing, and better rare drops are strong later, but they usually matter most after the core survival layer is already in place.

## 3. How To Choose Your Next Upgrade

- Ask what killed the last run first.
- If the answer is fuel, armor, or morale pressure, buy stability.
- If the answer is repeated combat failure after stable setup, buy damage or execution help.
- If the answer is neither, choose the upgrade that removes the most repeated friction.
