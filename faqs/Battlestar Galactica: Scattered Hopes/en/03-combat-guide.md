# Battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes Combat Guide

![Battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes official Steam trailer still showing tactical fleet action](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2535950/257309469/c1b2859def3761ca0bf832fa8132fd9d4df2a022/movie_600x337.jpg)

New players often enter combat trying to kill everything on screen. That is usually the wrong goal. In the early game, combat is mostly about protecting the fleet long enough to keep your route alive.

## Direct Answer

Play the early battles like defensive retreats. Pause immediately, send squadrons forward to block Raider pressure, keep civilian ships protected behind the main formation, focus dangerous heavy targets with your Gunstar, and jump as soon as FTL is ready instead of chasing a full clear.

## 1. Set Up Before Combat

### Enter Battle With Armor, Ammo, And Squadron Readiness

If the Gunstar is damaged, the squadrons are thin, or ammo is low, combat becomes much harder before the first order is even issued. Do not treat preparation as optional.

### Use A Protective Formation

Keep the main Gunstar central, put civilian ships behind or beside the protected line, and let squadrons form the first layer of contact. Early combat becomes much cleaner when you do not give Raiders easy angles.

## 2. The Simple Battle Flow

### Pause Immediately

Your first pause is the most valuable one because the situation is still recoverable. Use it to read the approach, assign interceptors, and stop the fight from becoming messy.

### Clear Raider Pressure First

Raiders punish bad positioning fast. Use squadrons to intercept them before they start chewing through vulnerable ships or forcing your main fleet out of shape.

### Focus The Real Threats

The Gunstar should pressure the target that matters most, not chase every low-health ship on the edge of the field. Heavy threats and momentum swings matter more than cleanup damage.

## 3. Common Early Mistakes

### Chasing Kills Too Far

If you break formation just to finish damaged enemies, you often open the fleet to worse losses than the kill was worth.

### Sending Every Squadron Forward

A full commit feels aggressive, but it leaves no defensive reserve. Keep enough squadron presence near the core fleet to answer flanks or return pressure.

### Staying After FTL Is Ready

Many runs die because players try to turn a safe escape into a prettier win. Once FTL is ready and the fleet can leave, leaving is usually the correct play.
