# Battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes Beginner Mistakes To Avoid

![Battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes official Steam header artwork](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2535950/d29dc74a212e14249b34ebbcdf028bbec5ce7ff0/header.jpg?t=1777991759)

Most early losses are not mysterious. They are usually the same handful of mistakes repeated in slightly different ways.

## Direct Answer

The biggest beginner mistakes are spending fuel too freely, wasting turns on low-value events, letting morale fall for too long, overrepairing side ships, staying too long in combat, ignoring infiltration, accepting every refugee or event burden, and treating early roguelite losses like failure instead of progression.

## 1. Resource Mistakes

### Spending Tylium Too Casually

Fuel is route survival. Trading it away early for short-term comfort often creates a much worse failure later.

### Overinvesting In Civilian Repairs

If your main Gunstar is still vulnerable, side repairs can wait. Preserving the fleet's core combat power matters more than polishing every asset.

## 2. Turn And Crisis Mistakes

### Wasting Management Turns

Triggering every interesting event feels productive, but it often steals time from repair, fuel stabilization, and battle preparation.

### Ignoring Infiltration Or Morale Problems

Some threats become much more expensive if delayed. Infiltration and morale collapse are both easier to contain early than to recover from late.

## 3. Combat Mistakes

### Chasing Full Clears

Early combat is often a survival test, not a total destruction puzzle. Staying too long for extra kills can throw away a winning position.

### Overcommitting Squadrons

If every squadron is pushed forward, your fleet loses a defensive cushion and can unravel quickly when pressure shifts.

## 4. Progression Mistakes

### Unlocking Fancy Things Before Stable Things

A stronger baseline usually matters more than a spicier build. Early permanent upgrades should reduce collapse risk before they chase damage spikes.

### Treating Every Early Loss As Failure

Scattered Hopes is built around repetition and learning. If a loss teaches you where the run broke, it already helped the next attempt.
