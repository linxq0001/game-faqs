# Battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes Fleet Management Guide

![Battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes official Steam page background artwork](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/2535950/4a43a83c8bf5aeee2084591c79559a664f93dc6c/page_bg_raw.jpg?t=1777991759)

Fleet management is where new runs usually become chaotic. The trick is not doing more. It is doing the right things in the right order while the turn count stays limited.

## Direct Answer

Use each management phase with a strict priority: secure the resources that keep the fleet alive, repair the main Gunstar, refill combat readiness, then spend any remaining turns on crisis handling or upgrades. If a choice does not help survival, stability, or the next battle, it can usually wait.

## 1. A Safe Turn Priority

### Open With Core Resource Sweeps

Your first management actions should usually go toward green Tylium points and stable supply gains. That keeps the route open and protects morale before you start spending turns elsewhere.

### Repair The Main Gunstar Early

A damaged main ship makes every later decision worse. Use salvage to keep the Gunstar ready before you invest in side repairs or nonessential modules.

### Refill Ammo And Check Squadron Status

Fleet management is not only about resources on paper. It is also your last chance to avoid entering combat underprepared. Treat squadron status and ammo as part of management, not something to remember later.

## 2. Crisis Priorities

### Handle Infiltration Before It Snowballs

Cylon infiltration is the kind of threat that looks manageable right up until it is not. Once it appears, spend the turn and resources to investigate instead of hoping it resolves itself.

### Stabilize Morale Before It Breaks Other Systems

Low morale does not stay isolated. It turns into crew problems, work disruption, and political instability. A moderate supply spend is usually cheaper than recovering from a full collapse.

### Accept That Not Every Problem Gets Solved At Once

Part of strong fleet management is controlled neglect. If a low-threat problem can wait while you solve a fuel, armor, or crisis emergency, let it wait.

## 3. Beginner Fleet Choices

### Start With Callisto

Callisto is the easiest place to learn the management loop because it is forgiving. It lets you make a few imperfect choices without instantly turning the next battle into disaster.

### Avoid High-Risk Fleet Experiments Too Early

More specialized ships can be powerful later, but they ask for stronger resource discipline and better crisis reading. Use them once you already understand the basic loop.
